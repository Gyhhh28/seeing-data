# About me
Hi! I'm Yuhe, a MISM student at CMU. I like finding meaning in data and spend an unreasonable amount of time contemplating the meaninglessness of my existence.

# What I hope to learn

1. Learn how to turn complex data and analysis into visualizations that are clear, intuitive, and actually tell a story.
2. Develop a better sense of visual design, especially how choices like color, layout, and emphasis can change the way people understand the same data.
3. Get better at communicating analytical insights to people who may not have a technical background.
